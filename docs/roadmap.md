## Next 10 Architectural Improvements

1. **Native On-Device Speech Transcription**: Integrate WebAssembly-based Whisper (Whisper.cpp) for in-browser client-side voice transcription without audio upload.
2. **Satellite & Drone Imagery Verification**: Automated computer vision pipeline comparing before/after satellite or drone imagery for large civic projects (e.g. road resurfacing).
3. **Automated WhatsApp / Telegram Grievance Bot**: Headless chat interface allowing citizens to file complaints and receive status updates directly via messaging apps.
4. **Geospatial GIS Heatmapping**: Mapbox/Leaflet clustering of grievances across municipal wards to automatically detect recurring pipe failures or infrastructure blackspots.
5. **Decentralized Verification DAO**: Community-voted peer-verification where nearby citizens can upvote or confirm whether a public road pothole was genuinely fixed.
6. **Cross-Departmental Dispute Arbiter**: Bounded arbitration workflow when multiple civic agencies (e.g., Telecom trenching vs. Road paving) dispute repair responsibility.
7. **Tamil Audio TTS Synthesis**: Natural Tamil voice response synthesis reading out case updates for illiterate or visually impaired citizens.
8. **Automated Contractor Penalty Calculations**: Automatic penalty deductions for civic maintenance contractors who breach SLA target deadlines.
9. **Zero-Knowledge Proofs for Citizen Verification**: ZK-SNARK verification of citizen municipal tax residency without disclosing citizen identity or address.
10. **Federated Learning for Grievance Routing**: Privacy-preserving model training across municipal corporations without sharing raw citizen grievance text.

- **Automated Contradiction Detection in Municipal Work Order Closures**: Evaluating textual vs photographic alignment in public sector contractor verification.

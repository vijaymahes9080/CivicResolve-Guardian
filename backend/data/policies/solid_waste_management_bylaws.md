# Municipal Solid Waste Management & Public Cleanliness Bylaws

**Document ID**: POL-WASTE-002  
**Department**: Solid Waste Management  
**Jurisdiction**: Tamil Nadu Urban Local Bodies  
**Version**: 2026.1  
**Default SLA**: 24 Hours (Garbage Dump Clearance), 72 Hours (Debris)  

---

### Section 2.1: Overflowing Community Bins & Open Garbage Dumping
- **Scope**: Uncollected street garbage bins, roadside dumping spots, foul odor, and dog/cattle nuisance.
- **Priority Level**: HIGH
- **SLA Target**: 24 hours for mechanical compactor clearing and lime-powder disinfection.
- **Evidence Required**: Photo of uncollected garbage bin or waste mound, GPS coordinate or street name.
- **Escalation Path**: Ward Sanitary Inspector (SI).

### Section 2.2: Illegal Construction & Demolition (C&D) Debris Dumping
- **Scope**: Dumping of concrete rubble, bricks, and construction earth on pedestrian footpaths or carriageways.
- **Priority Level**: MEDIUM
- **SLA Target**: 72 hours for notice issuance and municipal truck clearance.
- **Evidence Required**: Photo showing debris obstruction, approximate volume.
- **Escalation Path**: Assistant Town Planning Officer.

### Section 2.3: Dead Animal Removal on Public Thoroughfares
- **Scope**: Carcasses of stray animals obstructing roads or creating imminent health hazards.
- **Priority Level**: CRITICAL
- **SLA Target**: 6 hours for veterinary sanitization team pickup.
- **Evidence Required**: Location description, proximity to residential gates.
- **Escalation Path**: Chief Veterinary Officer.

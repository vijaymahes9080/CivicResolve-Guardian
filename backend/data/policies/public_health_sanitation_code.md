# Public Health, Vector Control & Sanitation Code

**Document ID**: POL-HEALTH-005  
**Department**: Public Health & Sanitation  
**Jurisdiction**: Tamil Nadu Urban Municipalities  
**Version**: 2026.1  
**Default SLA**: 24 Hours (Vector Fogging), 48 Hours (Food Stall Inspection)  

---

### Section 5.1: Mosquito Breeding in Stagnant Water & Dengue Outbreak Risk
- **Scope**: Stagnant stormwater pools, abandoned construction pits breeding Aedes mosquitoes.
- **Priority Level**: HIGH / CRITICAL
- **SLA Target**: 24 hours for Abate chemical larvicide application and fogging drive.
- **Evidence Required**: Photo of water stagnation pool, street or ward reference.
- **Escalation Path**: City Health Officer (CHO).

### Section 5.2: Unhygienic Street Food Vendors & Contaminated Food
- **Scope**: Roadside eateries dumping effluent into open drains, uncertified food handling.
- **Priority Level**: MEDIUM
- **SLA Target**: 48 hours for food safety officer inspection and seizure of stale ingredients.
- **Evidence Required**: Location of food stall, photo of unhygienic conditions.
- **Escalation Path**: Designated Food Safety Officer.

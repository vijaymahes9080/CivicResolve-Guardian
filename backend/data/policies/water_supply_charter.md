# Tamil Nadu Municipal Water Supply & Sewerage Charter

**Document ID**: POL-WATER-001  
**Department**: Water Supply & Sewerage Board  
**Jurisdiction**: Tamil Nadu Urban Municipalities  
**Version**: 2026.1  
**Default SLA**: 24 Hours (Contamination), 48 Hours (Pipeline Burst)  

---

### Section 1.1: Drinking Water Contamination & Discoloration
- **Scope**: Reports of discolored, turbid, sewage-mixed, or foul-smelling piped drinking water.
- **Priority Level**: CRITICAL / HIGH
- **SLA Target**: 24 hours for inspection, water sample testing, and alternative drinking water tanker deployment.
- **Evidence Required**: Clear photograph/video of tap water, exact residential landmark, ward number.
- **Escalation Path**: Assistant Executive Engineer (AEE), Water Quality Division.

### Section 1.2: Main Pipeline Burst & Heavy Leakage
- **Scope**: High-pressure pipeline fractures causing localized flooding or loss of municipal water supply.
- **Priority Level**: HIGH
- **SLA Target**: 12 hours for valve isolation, 48 hours for trenching and pipe replacement.
- **Evidence Required**: Photo of street leakage site, nearest cross-street or junction.
- **Escalation Path**: Maintenance Executive Engineer.

### Section 1.3: Underground Sewerage Overflow & Choked Manholes
- **Scope**: Overflowing sewage manholes posing severe biological hazard on public streets.
- **Priority Level**: CRITICAL
- **SLA Target**: 12 hours for super-sucker de-silting dispatch and chemical bleaching application.
- **Evidence Required**: Photo of overflowing manhole, street name.
- **Escalation Path**: Public Health & Sanitation Joint Command.

# Municipal Road Infrastructure & Pothole Repair Regulations

**Document ID**: POL-ROAD-003  
**Department**: Roads & Bridges  
**Jurisdiction**: Tamil Nadu Municipal Corporations  
**Version**: 2026.1  
**Default SLA**: 48 Hours (Hazardous Potholes), 7 Days (Footpath Repairs)  

---

### Section 3.1: Hazardous Road Potholes & Crater Formation
- **Scope**: Deep road craters (>5cm depth) endangering two-wheeler commuters and vehicular traffic.
- **Priority Level**: HIGH / CRITICAL (if on arterial road or near schools)
- **SLA Target**: 48 hours for cold-mix bitumen patching; 24 hours if classified as severe traffic safety hazard.
- **Evidence Required**: Photo showing pothole depth and relative road width, nearest landmark.
- **Escalation Path**: Assistant Engineer (AE) Roads.

### Section 3.2: Missing Stormwater Drain Grates & Footpath Slabs
- **Scope**: Open trench or damaged footpath concrete slab exposing pedestrian fall hazard.
- **Priority Level**: CRITICAL
- **SLA Target**: 12 hours for safety barricade placement, 36 hours for cast-iron grate replacement.
- **Evidence Required**: Photo of open drain, landmark.
- **Escalation Path**: Executive Engineer (Special Projects).

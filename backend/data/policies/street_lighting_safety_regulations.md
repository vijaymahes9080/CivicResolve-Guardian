# Municipal Street Lighting & Electrical Safety Code

**Document ID**: POL-LIGHT-004  
**Department**: Electrical & Street Lighting  
**Jurisdiction**: Tamil Nadu Municipal Corporations  
**Version**: 2026.1  
**Default SLA**: 24 Hours (Dark Stretches), 6 Hours (Hanging Live Wires)  

---

### Section 4.1: Street Light Non-Functionality & Dark Stretches
- **Scope**: Burnt LED fixtures, feeder pillar timer faults causing complete street darkness at night.
- **Priority Level**: MEDIUM / HIGH (if women's safety or bus-stop corridor)
- **SLA Target**: 24 hours for luminaire replacement or phase line rectification.
- **Evidence Required**: Pole identification number (stencil on pole), street name.
- **Escalation Path**: Junior Engineer (Electrical).

### Section 4.2: Hanging Electrical Wires & Exposed Junction Boxes
- **Scope**: Snapped cables, open junction box covers with live voltage accessible to pedestrians or children.
- **Priority Level**: CRITICAL
- **SLA Target**: 2 hours for electrical isolation, 6 hours for permanent insulated box sealing.
- **Evidence Required**: Photo of hanging wire or open junction box, exact pole position.
- **Escalation Path**: Superintending Engineer (Power Distribution).
